# Figure legends (manuscript version 7)

Graphical Abstract. Candidate microRNA relationships with O-GlcNAc-related enzymes in breast cancer therapy resistance. Nutrient excess increases flux through the hexosamine biosynthetic pathway and the supply of UDP-GlcNAc, the donor substrate of OGT and EOGT. OGT and OGA cycle O-GlcNAc on nuclear and cytoplasmic proteins, and O-GlcNAcylation of effectors such as ERα, TOP2A, MITF, and RUNX1 has been linked to reduced response to tamoxifen, doxorubicin, and palbociclib. EOGT modifies EGF-like domains in the endoplasmic reticulum lumen; its role in breast cancer resistance is a hypothesis. Of twelve microRNA-enzyme pairs evaluated, miR-200c-3p/EOGT is the priority candidate: it shows the strongest inverse correlation in TCGA-BRCA (Spearman rho = -0.25, FDR = 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁶, n = 1076), which persists after adjustment for tumor purity and PAM50 subtype (partial rho = -0.19), and EOGT is increased in tamoxifen-resistant cells (log2 fold change = +0.66, FDR 0.018). miR-200c-3p was lower in resistant LY2 cells in two array batches, with very different magnitudes (log2 fold change = -0.82, not significant, and -5.85, FDR < 0.05). Interpretation is limited because EOGT and miR-200c-3p were measured in different cell models, miR-200c-3p has no predicted site in the EOGT 3' untranslated region, direct targeting was not tested, EOGT is lower in tumor than in normal tissue, and no association with overall survival was observed. The relationships are node-specific candidates for functional validation and do not constitute a global rheostat.

Figure 1. Evidence map of candidate microRNA relationships with O-GlcNAc-related enzymes in breast cancer drug resistance. The twelve tested microRNA-enzyme pairs are shown for O-GlcNAc transferase (OGT) and O-GlcNAcase (OGA, also known as MGEA5), which cycle O-GlcNAc on nuclear and cytoplasmic proteins, and for EGF domain-specific O-GlcNAc transferase (EOGT), which modifies EGF-like domains in the endoplasmic reticulum lumen and is not part of OGT/OGA cycling. Connectors encode co-expression in 1076 TCGA-BRCA primary tumors (Spearman rho; Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the 12 pairs): solid blue bar-headed lines, inverse correlation with FDR < 0.05, which is compatible with repression but does not demonstrate targeting; dashed red lines, positive correlation with FDR < 0.05, opposite to a repression model; dotted gray lines, not significant. A black circle marked L indicates a direct interaction confirmed by luciferase reporter assay, in breast cancer for miR-24-3p/OGT (68) and in colorectal and ovarian cancer for miR-7-5p/OGT (56) and miR-181d-5p/OGT (57), respectively. Box outlines indicate the drug context in which each microRNA was reported (cyan, tamoxifen; purple, doxorubicin). The line under each name gives the log2 fold change in resistant versus sensitive cells in the present reanalysis. For enzymes, values are from GSE26459 (FDR, limma Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted p value; ns, FDR of 0.05 or above). For microRNAs, the two values are LY2 versus MCF-7 in the two array batches of GSE28267 (GPL7722 | GPL7723), read from the probe of the named arm; an asterisk marks FDR below 0.05. The star and crimson connector mark the priority candidate, miR-200c-3p/EOGT, which showed the strongest inverse correlation (rho = -0.25, FDR = 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁶) together with increased EOGT in the resistant model (log2 fold change = +0.66, FDR 0.018). miR-200c-3p has no predicted site in the EOGT 3' untranslated region (TargetScanHuman 8.0; a weak non-conserved site exists for miR-200c-5p), the values shown are precursor-level (arm-specific quantification assigned the miR-200c correlation to the 3p arm), and direct targeting was not tested; the relationship is a hypothesis for functional validation.

Figure 2. Integrative evaluation of candidate microRNA-enzyme relationships in TCGA-BRCA. (A) Spearman correlation between each candidate microRNA and its tested target enzyme across 1076 primary breast tumors with paired microRNA and messenger RNA profiles. Color encodes the correlation coefficient (blue, negative; red, positive). Asterisks denote statistical significance after Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all tested pairs (*, FDR < 0.05; **, FDR < 0.01; ***, FDR < 0.001) and mark significance in either direction; because color already encodes direction, only cells that are both blue (inverse) and starred are consistent with microRNA-mediated repression, whereas starred red cells are significant but in the opposite direction and do not support a repression model. Cells labeled n/t were not tested. The gold outline marks the priority candidate, the miR-200c-3p to EOGT relationship, which shows the strongest inverse correlation of all tested pairs but has no predicted site for miR-200c-3p in TargetScanHuman 8.0 and was not validated as a direct 3' untranslated region interaction in this study. (B) Hazard ratios for overall survival estimated by univariable Cox proportional-hazards regression, with each microRNA or enzyme entered as a continuous log2-transformed expression variable, so that each hazard ratio corresponds to a one-unit increase on the log2 scale. Points are hazard ratios and horizontal bars are 95 percent confidence intervals derived from the hazard ratio and its Wald p value; the dashed line marks a hazard ratio of one. Analyses were based on patients with available overall-survival follow-up (n = 1072 for the enzymes and n = 1055 for the microRNAs), with 150 deaths in the enzyme models and 146 deaths in the microRNA models. Red marks a nominal association (p < 0.05); however, no association remained significant after Benjamini-Hochberg correction across features, so these prognostic signals are suggestive rather than definitive. A multivariable model additionally adjusted for age and pathologic stage is reported in Supplementary Table S2 and did not alter these conclusions.
